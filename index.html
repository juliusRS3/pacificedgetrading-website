What is a PWA

An app that lives on the web, but feels like an app.

A progressive web app opens from a link and installs straight to the home screen. There's no app store, no approval wait, and every update reaches everyone instantly.

App-store app

Separate builds for iPhone and Android

  • Two codebases to build and maintain
  • Store review before every update
  • Staff must find and download it
  • Higher cost and longer timeline

Progressive web app

One app for every device

  • Works on iPhone, Android and desktop
  • Updates go live the moment we publish
  • Share a link, tap "Add to home screen"
  • Can keep working with a weak connection
