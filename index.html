What is a PWA

A website that works like an app

A progressive web app is built with web technology but behaves like a phone app. It is quicker and cheaper to make than a traditional app, and one version works on both iPhone and Android.

Traditional app

Built separately for iPhone and Android

  • Two apps to build and maintain
  • App Store and Google Play approval and fees
  • Users must search, download and update
  • Higher cost and longer build time

Progressive web app

What we build

  • One app for iPhone, Android and computer
  • No app store needed: share a link
  • Installs to the home screen in one tap
  • Updates instantly for every user

Examples

What a PWA can look like

Two examples of procurement apps that run as PWAs on phones and computers. Your app is designed around your own business and workflow.

Procurement platform

TenderHub: online procurement in one place

TenderHub is an example procurement PWA that replaces messy email threads with a structured workflow. Buyers create requests for quotation (RFQs), suppliers compete with their bids, and the best bid wins.

  1. Create RFQ
  2. Suppliers bid
  3. Compare bids
  4. Award
  5. Contract
  • Structured RFQs. Every request has the same clear scope, documents and closing date.
  • Supplier competition. Suppliers submit bids online instead of by email.
  • Side-by-side comparison. Buyers see every bid together and award the best one.
  • Full record. Every step from RFQ to contract is saved for audit.

Need something similar for your company? Tell us about it in the enquiry form.

Tender listing website

Tender Gateway: open tenders, straight from the source

Tender Gateway gathers open tenders from oil and gas companies and government bodies into one free list. Each entry links directly to the tender as advertised by the issuing company, so businesses find opportunities without checking dozens of websites.

  • Many sources, one list. Tenders from operators and governments across several countries.
  • Straight to the source. Every listing links to the official tender page. No documents are copied.
  • Free to browse. Anyone can search open tenders without signing up.
  • Bid support. Bidders can request consultancy to help prepare their proposal.
